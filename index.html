FAQ

Questions? We got you.

Is it really free?

Yes. Listing, buying, selling and messaging are all free. We will never take a cut of your sales.

Who can join?

Current HBCU students with a valid school email. Each campus has its own market, so you only trade with people at your school.

How do payments work?

You settle up however you're comfortable, whether that's cash at the meetup or your favorite payment app. The Yard Market never holds your money.

How do you keep it safe?

Student-only verification, in-app chat (so you never have to share your number), suggested public campus meetup spots, and one-tap reporting.

When is it launching?

We're rolling out campus by campus. Join the waitlist and tell us your school. The campuses with the most sign-ups go first.
